# Hæ, I’m Davíð

I’m the founder of [Upperblue](https://www.upperblue.com/), where I build maps and APIs for people working with Icelandic environmental data.

I spent eight years at the Icelandic Met Office, working on the systems that issue weather warnings and deliver forecasts. I later led data development at Iceland’s National Energy Authority. Today I work with much of that same data from the other side of the API.

- Look up the weather where you are in Iceland on [Upperblue Fast](https://fast.upperblue.com/en/).
- Map a day of earthquakes or plot Reykjavík’s tides with the [Upperblue API demos in Python](https://github.com/Upperblue-ehf/upperblue-api-demo).
- See my work on [data infrastructure, GIS and custom software](https://www.upperblue.com/en/services).
